🔒 About the App

The Women Safety App is designed to help women feel safer by offering emergency reporting, live location tracking, and quick contact features. It allows users to trigger SOS alerts to their emergency contacts and nearby help centers instantly.

👩‍💻 About the Creators

This project is developed by a team of passionate students/developers committed to women's safety and security. The goal is to use technology to make emergency assistance faster and more accessible.
