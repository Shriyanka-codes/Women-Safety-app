⚖️ Legal Rights for Women in India

Every woman in India is protected under the law. Here are a few key rights:

  • 🚨 **Right to file a complaint at any police station** (Zero FIR)
  • 👩‍⚖️ **Section 354 IPC** – Protection from assault or criminal force
  • 📞 **Helpline Numbers**: 1091 (Women), 181 (Legal aid), 100 (Police)
  • 👮 **Right to privacy during statement** – You can demand a female officer

📞 Helpline Numbers

  • 📱 1091 – Women's Helpline
  • 📱 181 – Women in Distress Legal Helpline
  • 📱 100 – Police
  • 📱 112 – National Emergency Number
